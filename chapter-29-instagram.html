ottawacentaursTHE OTTAWA CENTAURS ARE YOUR 2022 MLH CHAMPIONS

After a difficult first period ending with Colorado leading 2-0, our Centaurs came back with a vengeance and evened the score in the second, with a goal from Haas and another one from our captain Rozanov. Hollander scored two goals in the third, Colorado did the same, and Hollander completed the hat trick 45 seconds into overtime, winning game 5 and the cup for the Centaurs! Congratulations to the whole team for a record-breaking season!

Enjoy a well-deserved break, we’ll see you in September for the preseason, and stay tuned for some exciting announcements!
